Privacy Policy

Neon Lasso: Loop Arcade · Effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information Neon Lasso: Loop Arcade ("the game") handles. The game is made by Nova Games ("we").

No accounts, no personal profile

The game has no sign-up and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell personal information.

Game progress

Your scores, gems, upgrades and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard and is shown with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can deliver the item.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data

Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a daily reminder on your device. No information is sent to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
