Contact support

Found a bug or have a question? Email nova.support@novagamestudioapp.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How to play

Slide your finger to move your light. Draw a loop around enemies and close it to destroy everything inside. Collect crystals to charge NOVA, then tap the glowing bar to clear the screen. You can replay the tutorial any time with HOW TO PLAY on the home screen.

Purchases

To restore No Ads on a new device, open the shop, go to GEMS and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID. Gems are stored on the device where they were bought.
